# Compact quotation sections

## Scope
- Keep the current quotation viewer, masthead, branding, quote data, calculations, and controls unchanged.
- Refine only the client details/summary and annuity option sections.

## Changes
- Arrange client details in two deliberate columns: Date of Birth, ID / Passport Number, and Email on the left; Gender and Contact on the right.
- Place Funeral Cover and Purchase Premium in a compact two-column summary row beneath the client fields.
- Retain thin field rules, small labels, and aligned values without introducing cards.
- Reshape each annuity option to the older quotation’s compact bordered block: combined option title, compact living-annuity rows, four-column guarantee table, and italic zero-escalation note.
- Apply the same structure and density to screen and PDF styles.

## Export safeguards
- Keep A4 size, 12 mm print margins, unbroken option blocks, and terms-flow rules.
- Use the compact export mode for one through four options so they fit within two A4 pages; allow additional pages only beyond four options.
- Verify the preview, generated markup, build result, and PDF-specific pagination rules without changing business logic.
